**LAB 22** is the research laboratory directed by **Prof. Gabriele Trovato** at the **Shibaura Institute of Technology (SIT)** in Tokyo, Japan.  
We focus on innovation through interdisciplinarity, bridging engineering, humanities, and design.

---

### 🔬 Core Research Fields

- **Social Robotics & Human-Robot Interaction (HRI)**
- **Robot Aesthetics & Embodied Interaction**
- **Religion and Culture in Robotics & AI**
- **Procedural Content Generation (PCG)**

---

### 🤖 Robotic Platforms & Embodiments

Our research involves custom-built interactive machines, cultural agents, and commercial humanoid systems:

| Robot / Device | Category | Role & Field |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SanTO** / **ClémenCE** | Cultural & Social Robot | Robotic platforms investigating religion, theology, and prayer interaction in AI. |
| **DarumaTO Series** (3, 4W, 5) | Social Companion & Coach | Health coaching devices and affective interaction agents (e.g. *WABO*, *NAMIBO*). |
| **CelesTE** (R3, R4) | Coaching Device | Multimodal assistant for elderly assistance and active living. |
| **ToDo** | Plant Companion | Plant-inspired ambient companion robot. |
| **HIRO & HRP-2** | Full-scale Humanoids | Bipedal & dual-arm humanoid platforms (Kawada Robotics). |

---

### 📌 Key Research Projects

- **顔身体デザイン (Face & Body Design):**  
  *JSPS Transformative Research Area (A)* — Designing the future of artificial faces and bodies by integrating robotics with the humanities.
- **Religion in Robotics and AI:**  
  Investigating the convergence between machines, sacred interaction, and cultural perception through robots exhibited worldwide in museums, churches, and universities.
- **e-ViTA (EU-Japan Horizon 2020 / MIC Japan):**  
  Virtual coaching system promoting active and healthy living for elderly independence through social robots, affective computing, and IoT.
